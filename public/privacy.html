Privacy Policy — Minto Farm Records

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Minto Farm Records ("the app") is a private record-keeping tool operated by Minto Pastoral Co for use by its staff and authorised contractors. This policy explains what information the app holds and how it is used. The app is an internal business tool; it is not intended for the general public or for children.

Information we collect

  • Account details — the email address and password used to sign in. Accounts are created by Minto Pastoral management; the app does not allow public sign-up.
  • Farm records you enter — livestock mobs and numbers, paddock and property information, stock movements, treatments, rainfall, wool, purchase orders, musters, menus and similar operational records.
  • Messages and photos you post in the in-app team chat.
  • Activity information — a log of actions (e.g. who recorded or deleted an entry) tied to the signed-in account, for accountability.

How we use it

The information is used solely to run Minto Pastoral's farm records and coordinate work between staff. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with third parties for their own purposes.

Storage and security

  • Data is stored in a secured database (Supabase / PostgreSQL) hosted in Australia.
  • Access requires signing in with an authorised account; the database is protected so that only authenticated staff accounts can read or write data.
  • Backups are taken to prevent data loss.

Who can see the data

Records and chat are visible to other signed-in staff and contractors of Minto Pastoral. The activity log is available to management only.

Device permissions

If you add a photo to the team chat, the app uses your device's camera or photo library only for that purpose, and only when you choose to attach a photo.

Retention

Records are kept for as long as they are useful to the business. Accounts can be removed by management at any time; removing an account revokes that person's access.

Your requests

If you are a staff member and would like your account removed or your personal information addressed, contact us using the details below.

Changes
