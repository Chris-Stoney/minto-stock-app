Minto Farm Records — Quick Guide

The farm stock book, on your phone. app.mintopastoral.com.au

Getting started

  1. Open app.mintopastoral.com.au on your phone.
  2. Add it to your home screen so it opens like an app — iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: ⋮ → Add to home screen.
  3. Tap the green M icon and sign in with the login you were given.
  4. Go to Chat and type your name once, so everyone knows who's who.

The tabs along the bottom

Yards — totals & alerts Stock — mobs by paddock Rain Wool Chat — team + photos Recs — every record Setup — managers

Pick a property from the drop-down top-right to filter everything to that place. The orange + Record button (on the Yards screen) is the fastest way to log anything.

Purchase order+ Record → Purchase order → details → Save

Good to know

  • Numbers update themselves. Move, sell, mark, or wean and the head counts and paddocks change automatically.
  • Made a mistake? Go to Recs (or Recent activity on Yards), find it, tap the ✕ — it undoes the entry and puts the numbers back.
  • ⚠ WHP means do NOT sell yet. A red WHP/ESI note means that mob is still inside a withholding period after treatment.
  • 🐂 Receiving yards on the Yards screen = stock trucked in that needs a paddock — tap Allocate.
  • Everyone sees the same data, live. Type carefully — it's the real stock book.
